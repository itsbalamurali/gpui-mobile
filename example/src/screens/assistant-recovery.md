### Keep the work, explain the interruption

If a reply fails halfway through, preserve what has already arrived. Place the error beside that reply so the context stays clear.

**Suggested message**

> The reply was interrupted. Your conversation is saved. Try again when you’re ready.

Offer **Retry** as the primary action. Keep **Copy** available for the partial answer.

#### A small detail that matters

Use ~~Something went wrong~~ **The connection was interrupted** when that is the cause you actually know. Specific language makes the next step easier to understand.

Never clear the user’s draft just because a request failed.
